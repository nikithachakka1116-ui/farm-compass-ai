# 🌱 KisanLeaf – AI-Powered Smart Farming Assistant

KisanLeaf is an AI-powered agricultural web application designed to help farmers identify crop diseases and make better farming decisions using AI, weather and field information.

## 🎯 Problem

Farmers often face crop diseases, unpredictable weather, irrigation problems and limited access to agricultural experts.

KisanLeaf acts as a real-time bridge between field conditions and expert agronomic guidance.

## 💡 Solution

KisanLeaf converts real-world farm information into simple and actionable recommendations.

**Crop Photo + Location + Weather → AI Analysis → Risk Assessment → Actionable Advisory**

## 🧠 AI Workflow

1. Farmer uploads a crop or leaf image.
2. AI analyzes the image.
3. Probable disease and confidence are displayed.
4. Crop severity and risk are calculated.
5. Farmer location is used for local weather information.
6. Live weather and forecast data are analyzed.
7. Treatment recommendations are generated.
8. Safe action or spraying time is calculated.
9. Irrigation requirements are estimated.
10. Treatment cost is estimated.
11. Farmer receives clear action steps.
12. Follow-up images can monitor crop recovery.

## 🔐 Safety & Trust

KisanLeaf is a decision-support system and does not replace agricultural experts.

The system provides AI confidence levels and recommends expert verification when confidence is low.

Chemical treatment guidance should always be verified with product labels and local agricultural authorities.
